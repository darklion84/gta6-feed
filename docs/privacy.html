Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This site is an unofficial fan project that collects links to short GTA 6 clips published on other platforms and shows them in one feed. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. The clips are not hosted here: they are played by the platforms' own embedded players.

Embedded players and their cookies

Clips are played by the official embedded players of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Telegram. When a player loads, the platform behind it can set cookies or similar storage in your browser and receives technical data such as your IP address, browser details and the page you are on. We do not control this processing; it is governed by each platform's own policy:

  • YouTube (Google): Google Privacy Policy
  • TikTok: TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Instagram (Meta): Meta Privacy Policy
  • Telegram: Telegram Privacy Policy

No player is loaded until you choose Watch clips on your first visit. If you decline, the feed shows links to the clips on their original platforms instead.

YouTube API Services

This site uses YouTube API Services to find public clips and to read their public details: title, channel name, length and view, like and comment counts. By watching YouTube clips here you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not ask you to sign in and never access any YouTube account data. Public clip details are refreshed regularly and deleted within 14 days.

What this site stores in your browser

We set no cookies of our own. The site keeps a few values in your browser's local storage so that it works the way you left it; they never leave your device:

  • whether you allowed embedded players;
  • the clip you stopped at, so the feed resumes there;
  • whether you switched the sound on.

You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Visit statistics

To see how many people visit the site we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts page views in aggregate, sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not track individual visitors across sites. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by a hosting provider, which processes your IP address and request details in its server logs in order to deliver the pages and protect the service. We have no accounts, no sign-up forms and no comment sections, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Removal requests and contact

If you are the author of a clip and want it removed from the feed, or you have a privacy question, contact us on Telegram: @darklion84.
